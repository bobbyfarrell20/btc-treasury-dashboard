Notes
Solid = all-in (EV) mNAV: (price × basic shares + debt + preferred notional − cash) ÷ BTC NAV. Default view emphasizes all-in (EV); the faint dotted line is market-standard mNAV (context; full weight in "market standard" / "both"): price × basic shares ÷ BTC NAV. Diluted equity mNAV (price × assumed-diluted shares ÷ BTC NAV) is hidden; click it in the legend to show it. Dashed lines: 1.0x and the overheated floor (config).
